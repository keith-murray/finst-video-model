# Do VLMs have FINST-like object tracking mechanisms?

Video language models (VLMs) have made large strides over the past two years. VLMs can now digest hour long videos and provide relevant descriptions of the video. This raises the question, "What are the emergent mechanisms in VLMs that allow them to reason about videos?" To constrain this question, we can lean on the work of Zenon Pylyshyn to ask, "How do VLMs track multiple objects?"

Pylyshyn's work proposes an index-like mechanism that agents assign to objects moving in the environment termed (F)ingers of (INST)antiation (aka FINST). Do VLMs have a FINST-like mechanism to track objects?
